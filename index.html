Int. The story so far — Day

The story
so far.

Hi, I'm Shankar. I'm in the first year of my B.Tech in Information Technology, so I'm new enough that most days still teach me something I didn't know existed.

I came to IT curious about how the apps and sites I use every day are actually made. Behind every screen, someone made a hundred small decisions. I want to be one of those people.

Right now I'm learning the basics properly instead of rushing past them. I would rather understand one thing well than copy ten things I can't explain.

This site is my first real attempt at making something that feels designed. It's one file with no images, and it's still teaching me things.
